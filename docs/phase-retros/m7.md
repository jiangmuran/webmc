## Shipped

- `src/items/item.ts` + `Inventory.ts`: ItemDef registry + 9 hotbar + 27 main + 4 armor + offhand slots, merge-into-existing-then-fill-empty, stack-size-aware, hotbar selection clamp.
- `src/items/recipe.ts`: shaped (with optional horizontal mirror) + shapeless resolver with bag-equality. Bounding-box extraction of non-null grid cells so 2×2 and 3×3 recipes share one resolver.
- `src/items/block-drops.ts`: BlockDropRegistry with tool-kind + tool-tier gating and rng-injectable count ranges.
- `src/game/PlayerState.ts`: 20 HP + 20 hunger + saturation with MC-equivalent decay/regen rules. Sprint × 4 drain, HP regen at hunger ≥ 18. Respawn clears inventory and calls a callback into main.ts that teleports the player back to spawn.
- `src/entities/mob.ts`: MobWorld + MOB_DEFS for pig / zombie / skeleton. Reuses sweepMove for physics, line-of-sight aggro within 16 m, damage on 1.5 m proximity with 0.8s cooldown (zombie), 8 m (skeleton).
- `src/entities/spawn.ts`: day-passive / night-hostile SpawnSystem with per-category caps (4 hostile / 6 passive), 5-24 m ring around player, automatic despawn beyond 2× max radius.
- `src/entities/pathfinding.ts`: voxel A\* with configurable jump-up (1) and fall-down (3) deltas, max-expansions budget, deterministic step costs.
- `src/engine/render/MobRenderer.ts`: placeholder coloured boxes per mob (pig pink, zombie green, skeleton gray) with per-frame sync and geometry reuse.
- `main.ts` wires it all: break-block → drops → inventory; SpawnSystem.tick; MobWorld.tick hooks into PlayerState.takeDamage; HUD gains HP / food / items / mobs counters.

## Biggest surprise

`noUncheckedIndexedAccess` pushed me to design the inventory around typed arrays of `(ItemStack | null)` rather than sparse arrays with `undefined` holes; the stricter model actually helps at the consumption site (null guard is explicit). Second surprise: even a small 4-direction A\* expands fast when dy range is -3..+1, so I pinned maxExpansions per call to bound pathological worst cases.
